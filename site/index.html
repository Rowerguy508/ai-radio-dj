Personal AI radio DJ for iPhone and iPad

Your music, with a DJ on air.

OnAirly: Personal AI Radio DJ turns Apple Music into a radio station that's yours. Pick what a station is built from, and a DJ talks between the songs: back-announcing what just played and teeing up what's next.

How it works ON AIR

Build a station

Start a station from artists, songs, playlists or genres in Apple Music. Choose how far it reaches from those seeds, how much energy it has, and how familiar or new the picks are.

A DJ between songs

On some song changes, the DJ steps in: a greeting, a back-announce, a word on the artist, the station ID, and a short line about what's coming up next.

Set the tone

Pick a personality (warm, upbeat, laid back or minimal) and how often the DJ talks, from rarely to frequently. Or turn the talk off and just listen.

Your day, on air

An optional daily briefing plays the first time you listen each day. The DJ can mention what's coming up, using calendar events, Markdown notes and items you've shared to OnAirly and approved.

On your device

Your calendar, Markdown notes, shared items, music library and station settings are read and kept on your iPhone or iPad.

No tracking

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in OnAirly, and we don't sell or share your data.

On-device voices

The built-in voices speak entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

What you need

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, and an Apple Music subscription. OnAirly plays music through Apple Music using your own subscription, and is rated 13+.
