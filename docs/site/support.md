## Common questions

**Do I need Apple Music?**
Yes. OnAirly plays music through Apple Music using your own subscription. If playback doesn't start, check that you're signed in to Apple Music and that OnAirly has Media & Apple Music access in iOS Settings.

**The DJ isn't talking.**
Check that DJ commentary is on for the station and that your phone isn't muted. If the cloud voice isn't available, OnAirly falls back to the on-device voice. The Phonon voice needs a one-time download over Wi-Fi.

**Why does OnAirly want my calendar?**
So the DJ can mention what's coming up in your day. It's optional, the events stay on your iPhone, and you can turn access off in iOS Settings at any time.

**What leaves my phone?**
Nothing personal. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the full details. [PENDING: paid voice] With the cloud voice on, only song and artist names and fixed DJ phrasing are sent to make the voice.

**How do I turn the cloud voice off?** [PENDING: paid voice]
Go to Settings, then Voice, and switch the cloud voice off. Nothing more is sent after that.

**Subscriptions and refunds** [PENDING: paid voice]
Purchases are handled by Apple. To cancel, use iOS Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. To ask for a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). [owner: confirm product names and what happens to Pro voice after a refund.]

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete shared items in the app, or delete OnAirly to remove everything it stored on your iPhone. [owner, pending paid voice: how to ask for server-side deletion.]
