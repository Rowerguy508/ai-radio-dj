# OnAirly Support

OnAirly: Personal AI Radio DJ runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, and needs an Apple Music subscription.

## Contact us

Email [support@onairly.app](mailto:support@onairly.app). Replies come from onairly@agentmail.to, so please look for that address (and check your spam folder). Support replies within 3 business days. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version and OnAirly version. Never send us your Apple ID password or payment details.

## Common questions

**Do I need Apple Music?**
Yes. OnAirly plays music through Apple Music using your own subscription. If playback doesn't start, check that you're signed in to Apple Music and that OnAirly has Media & Apple Music access in iOS Settings.

**The DJ isn't talking.**
Check that DJ talk isn't set to Off for the station and that your device isn't muted.

**Why does OnAirly want my calendar?**
So the DJ can mention what's coming up in your day. It's optional, the events stay on your device, and you can turn access off in iOS Settings at any time.

**What leaves my device?**
Nothing personal. Your calendar, Markdown notes and shared items stay on your device. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the full details.

**Refunds**
Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete shared items in the app, stop using your calendar or Markdown notes in Connections, or delete OnAirly to remove everything it stored on your device.
